Selected work & publications

Research, instruments, and writing, with what each one was and where it landed.

What's Fair? Exploring the Behavioral Science of Justice and Fairness

Government Finance Officers Association. Lead author of a six-article research series with Alison Taylor and Shayne Kavanagh, translating behavioral science on fairness into practice for senior public-sector leaders. GFOA represents more than 21,000 public finance officers across the United States and Canada.

Ethical Culture Scale

Ethical Systems @ NYU Stern. Designed and validated a 20-item, 5-factor organizational measure through item and factor analysis. Released free for academic use.

Assessment Landscape Review

Ethical Systems @ NYU Stern. Built and maintained a systematic review of available organizational culture and ethics instruments, covering the theory, utility, and defensibility of each.

Speak-up behavior research

Corporate research engagement. Designed and deployed a study at a large corporation on what drives employees to report, or stay silent about, unethical behavior. Findings advanced the client's understanding of speak-up behavior inside their own organization.

SciRec pre-employment assessments

SciRec. Researched the underlying theory, then built and validated eight cognitive and behavioral assessments from the ground up in seven months, including item response theory, factor analysis, reliability, validity evidence, and bias and sensitivity testing.

Adaptability Assessment, development and validation

Consulting contract, T Labs. Part of a three-person team building a new pre-employment instrument. Led statistical analysis and validation, including reliability, factor structure, and criterion-related validity.

Navigating Ethical Systems: A Practitioner's Guide

Published professional guide. An individual- and firm-focused guide distilling research into practical guidance for professionals. Read the PDF.

Me and My TV: Reconceptualizing the Television Audience

Consulting contract, global telecom. Applied factor-analytic methods to build psychographic viewer profiles grounded in uses-and-gratifications theory. The resulting profiles improved content recommendation.

SPSS Linear Regression: Complete Tutorial

Udemy. Authored more than 11 hours of statistics instruction, serving 3,400+ students.
