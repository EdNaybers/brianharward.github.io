What I actually do

I turn organizational questions into measurement that holds up, then explain the results to the people who have to act on them. In practice that means three things.

Measurement and psychometrics

Assessment design and validation, item response theory, factor analysis, reliability, construct and criterion validity, DIF and measurement invariance, adverse impact and fairness analysis against EEOC and UGESP standards, job analysis, and competency modeling. Most recently, eight pre-employment assessments taken from theory through full validation in seven months.

People analytics and employee listening

Survey design, weighting, and scale development; engagement and driver analysis; culture and ethics measurement; selection and assessment programs; reporting that executives use rather than file.

Research science

Experimental and quasi-experimental design, mixed-effects and regression models, segmentation, conjoint, longitudinal analysis, and mixed methods. I have been the entire research function for a think tank at NYU Stern, owning projects from framing through executive presentation.

Tools

R, Python, SQL, SPSS, SAS, Stata, Mplus, BigQuery, Tableau, Power BI, Qualtrics, Excel.
